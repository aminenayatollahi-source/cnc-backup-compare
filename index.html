Comparison limits

Files are matched by relative path and compared with SHA-256 up to 64 MiB per file. Unreadable or oversized files appear as unchecked. Line comparison supports UTF-8 and BOM-marked UTF-16 text up to 128 KiB per file and 750,000 comparison cells. Extract compressed backups first. Proprietary ARC and PLC formats are not decoded.
